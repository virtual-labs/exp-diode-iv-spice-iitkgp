## References
#### Books
1) G. Massobrio and P. Antognetti, Semiconductor Device Modeling with SPICE, 2nd ed., McGraw-Hill, 1993. (This is a foundational text and has a dedicated chapter on the diode model, its parameters, and their extraction.)

2) S. M. Sze and Kwok K. Ng, Physics of Semiconductor Devices, 3rd ed., Wiley, 2006. (Provides the deep semiconductor physics background for the diode equation and the physical meaning of parameters like IS, N, and EG.)

3) Dieter K. Schroder, Semiconductor Material and Device Characterization, 3rd ed., Wiley-IEEE Press, 2006. (Chapter 4 specifically discusses I-V measurements and how to extract key parameters like ideality factor and series resistance from them.)

4) Paolo A. A. N. D. L. M. G., "Parameter Extraction," in Power Diode Design and Fabrication, Springer, 2021. (A more modern text that includes sections on parameter extraction, often using numerical and optimization methods.)
